About CyberDict

CyberDict is a community cybersecurity dictionary and interactive knowledge explorer for offensive security, defensive security, purple teaming and AI security.

MISSION

Make cybersecurity terminology easier to search, understand and connect without reducing technical topics to one-line definitions.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Search first. Structured explanations. Related concepts. Offensive and defensive context. Framework mappings where they add value. Open contribution paths.

PROJECT MODES

SEARCH finds terms quickly. EXPLORE connects concepts. LEARN turns categories into structured learning paths. ASK is reserved for a later AI-assisted phase.
