// ABOUT CYBERDICT

Security knowledge, made easier to navigate.

CyberDict is a community cybersecurity knowledge explorer designed to make security concepts easier to understand through concise explanations, visual learning and connected context.

01

WHAT IS CYBERDICT?

CyberDict brings cybersecurity terminology, techniques, controls and frameworks into one searchable reference. Each concept is structured to help readers move from a short definition to technical context, offensive and defensive relevance, visual explanation and related concepts.

02

WHY IT EXISTS

Cybersecurity knowledge is fragmented across standards, vendor documentation, frameworks, research and training material. CyberDict provides a practical learning layer that connects those concepts without trying to replace authoritative documentation.
